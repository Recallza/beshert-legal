Help us test Beshert

A Jewish dating app, about to launch on Android — and we need a few real people to try it first.

Before Beshert can go live on Google Play, Google requires us to run a short closed test with a handful of real testers for 14 days. We're a small, independent team building this — if you're willing to help, it takes about five minutes total over two weeks.

What's involved

  • You'll need an Android phone and a Google account.
  • We'll add your email to a private tester list and send you a one-time link.
  • You install the app from the Play Store like normal, and just open it a few times over the next two weeks (you don't need to actually use it as a dating app — genuine opens are all Google requires).
  • That's it. No cost, no commitment beyond that.

Use the Gmail address tied to the Google account you'll install the app with — that's the one we'll add to the tester list.
